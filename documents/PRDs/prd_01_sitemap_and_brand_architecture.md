---

### Typography Guidelines

- Font Style: Sans-serif, sharp, neutral (e.g., Inter / Helvetica-like)
- Tone: Institutional, controlled, non-promotional
- Heading Style:
  - Large, bold, minimal words
  - Avoid decorative styling
- Body Text:
  - Medium weight
  - High readability
- Line Height: 1.5–1.7 for body

#### Writing Rules
- No buzzwords (e.g., “revolutionize”, “transform”)
- Use system language:
  - “decisioning layer”
  - “data pipeline”
  - “policy-bound inference”
- Every statement must imply a system or process
